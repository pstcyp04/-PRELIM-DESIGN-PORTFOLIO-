🗂️✨ MY CREATIVE PORTFOLIO JOURNEY 🎨
This portfolio documents three design activities completed during the Prelim period.

🎨 Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

A complete visual identity study for PRSTN Flower Shop featuring coordinated colors and typography.

📊 Activity 3: Social Media Infographics
An informative infographic about Pax Silica in the Philippines that combines research, visual storytelling, and structured communication.

🎯 Overall Learning
These activities helped me understand that good design is not only about appearance. Effective design improves communication, helps audiences understand information, and creates meaningful visual experiences.
