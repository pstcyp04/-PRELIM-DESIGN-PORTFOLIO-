# ✍️✨ BEHIND THE DESIGN: MY CREATIVE PROCESS 🎨

## 1. Research and Content Gathering

I identified the important information and selected the key points that could be presented clearly.

## 2. Content Organization

The information was divided into numbered sections to create a logical reading flow.

## 3. Visual Direction

I used Philippine-inspired colors in the header and green and teal tones throughout the infographic to connect ideas of sustainability, development, and the environment.

## 4. Visual Elements

I added city, technology, transportation, and development-related illustrations to make the infographic more engaging.

## 5. Final Layout

Information boxes and numbered headings were used to create visual hierarchy and improve readability.
