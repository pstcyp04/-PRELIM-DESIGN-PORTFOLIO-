# 🎨 🎨🌈 THE COLOR STORY ✨

The selected palette supports the elegant and minimalist identity of PRSTN Flower Shop.

| Color | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| ⚫ Black | `#000000` | Sophistication and strong contrast |
| 🔵 Dark Navy | `#0F2647` | Trust, calmness, and premium identity |
| ⚪ Gray | `#737373` | Neutral balance |
| 🤍 Light Gray | `#D9D9D9` | Soft supporting elements |
| 🟤 Cream/Beige | `#DDD8C3` | Warmth and natural floral feeling |
| ⚪ White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean space and readability |

## Why These Colors?

The combination creates a sophisticated visual identity. The dark colors provide elegance and contrast, while the cream and light tones add warmth and softness.
