# 🔍 Design Analysis

## Visual Hierarchy

The largest text, **FREE COFFEE**, immediately attracts the viewer's attention. Supporting information is placed below it in smaller text.

## Color and Contrast

The combination of cream, blue, and dark tones creates readability and gives the design a calm coffee-shop aesthetic.

## Visual Elements

The coffee cup and coffee beans strengthen the concept and help communicate the subject without relying only on text.

## Reflection

This activity taught me that design principles are important because they guide how information is arranged and understood by an audience.
